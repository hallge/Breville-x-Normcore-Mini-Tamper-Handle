# Attribution and third-party files

## Original design

`files/tamper.scad` and `files/tamper.stl` are the unmodified originals of
**Breville Dual Boiler (BDB) Magnetic Tamper Handle for Normcore v4** by
Thingiverse user **vickeryj** -
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5976291 - CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. The original
author's `LICENSE.txt` and `README.txt` are kept alongside.

`files/tamper_snapin.scad`, `files/tamper_mini_flair.scad` and
`files/tamper_mini_noflair.scad` are adaptations of that design.

## Library

`files/threads.scad` - *threads-scad* by Ryan A. Colyer,
https://github.com/rcolyer/threads-scad, **CC0 1.0**. One local change:
`screw_resolution` is 0.4 instead of 0.2, which halves the thread facet count
and keeps full renders fast. The geometry difference is about 3 microns.

## Not included

Measurements for the Mini handle were taken from product images published by
**Normcore Wares**, and the base-flare proportions from a product photo by
**ARO Espresso**. Those images are their owners' copyright and are not in this
repository. The thread (M14 x 0.75) was cross-checked against two other
people's handle designs for the same tamper, which are likewise not
redistributed here:

- *stinkytamper* by rykb - https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5971191 (CC BY)
- *Normcore Tamper - Palm tamping handle* by RHKG -
  https://www.printables.com/model/1297451-normcore-tamper-palm-tamping-handle
